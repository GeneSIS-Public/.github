# GeneSIS

[日本語](./README.md)

> **Create continuity with AI on the human side.**

GeneSIS is an individually developed project for maintaining dialogue, thought, records, and knowledge created between humans and AI on the human side.

One GeneSIS-Operator develops and operates the project with AI assistance and makes final decisions concerning publication, conditions, and responses.

The `GeneSIS-Public` organization is the official area for publishing **technical information and official documents** concerning GeneSIS and Project: Code-NOAH.

## Project: Code-NOAH

Project: Code-NOAH addresses continuity with AI, records, reconnection, and structures managed on the human side.

### G.E.N.E

G.E.N.E is a local AI dialogue environment for Windows x64 that records and operates AI dialogue as local data.

G.E.N.E is not an LLM. It connects to an external local LLM environment, such as Ollama, and an available model.

- [Official G.E.N.E Repository](https://github.com/GeneSIS-Public/G.E.N.E)

The source code is not publicly released.
